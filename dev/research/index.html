Research record

The curated documentation teaches the current workflow. The repository also retains a detailed research ledger: calibration summaries, negative results, freeze inventories, recovery work, and experimental protocols. These records are valuable for audit and reproduction, but they are not an ordered beginner path.

Start with:

  • Mission and scope
  • Architecture
  • Testing and validation
  • Calibration results
  • Correctness recovery plan
  • Development roadmap
  • Prior art and shared terminology
  • Power diagnostics v2

Machine-generated JSON files in docs/ are evidence artifacts consumed by the research process. They are intentionally excluded from the site navigation. Frozen historical implementations and evaluations remain unchanged for replay; the site describes the current supported path.

Passing package tests establishes software contracts. It does not establish scientific calibration, transfer to unseen networks, physical validity, or human repair benefit. Those claims require their own data and study design.
